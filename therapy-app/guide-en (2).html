What if your therapist was
always with you?

Parents sit down with a therapist for counseling once or twice a week. The other six days are up to you. Therapy Buddy is a pocket-sized companion that helps you apply your child's goals at home — learning more about your child with every log to provide customized guidance designed to accelerate progress.

"I started thinking: what would it be like if I had access to my therapist 24/7 — in my pocket? I tried it with my son and it actually helped. I stopped forgetting his goals between sessions. And when a technique stopped working, I didn't have to wait until the next appointment to get new ideas."

— Sera Koo, mom of two & Therapy Buddy builder

Getting started — 3 minutes
1

Open the link and create a free account

Sign in with Google or enter your email to get a sign-in link. Your data will be saved and accessible from any device.

2

Add your child's goals

Add what you're already trying or want to try. Each one becomes an action card. You can always edit in the Goals tab.

3

Log what happens, as it happens

Tap a goal card, record what you tried and how it went. Takes under 2 minutes. The more you log, the better the recommendations get.

4

Add to your home screen for easy access

iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home Screen. No app store needed!

Each log has 3 parts
🔵
Before
Child's state
Was your child calm, focused, or resistant before you tried?
🟢
Treatment
What you tried
Which technique? What worked, even a little? What didn't?
🟡
After
Results
How did your child react? Success, partial success, or no success?
What's in this version
✓

Free account & multi-device sync — sign in with Google or email. Your data is saved securely and accessible from any device.

✓

Action cards — one per goal, with trigger, steps, and what to avoid. Gets updated automatically when you generate a new cycle.

✓

Weekly progress — check the "This Week" tab for success rates per goal, what's working, and what to try next.

✨

Next cycle recommendations — tap "Generate next cycle" in the Week tab. Your logs are analyzed and action cards updated automatically.

📋

Progress report — generate a report in the Week tab. Choose a time period and share via email with anyone you'd like.

📱

Home screen icon — add Therapy Buddy to your home screen for quick access, just like a native app.
