The appointments, the IEP meetings, the homework from therapists, the feeling that you should be doing more — it's a lot to carry. What Therapy Buddy does for you
💭

Vent it out. We'll organize it for you.

Had a hard day? Just talk. Open the app and say what happened — in your own words, in your own time. No forms to fill, no jargon. We'll turn it into a structured note automatically, so nothing gets lost.

📋

Walk into every meeting prepared.

Your next IEP meeting, therapy check-in, or teacher conference — you'll have something to show. Generate a progress report in seconds and share it directly from your phone. No more trying to remember everything on the spot.

✨

Know what's working — and what to try next.

As you log, Therapy Buddy learns what works for your child. When a technique stops helping, we'll suggest specific, concrete alternatives — real activities, real games, real moments you can try at home.

👨‍👩‍👧

Stay coordinated with your family members.

Invite a co-parent, grandparent, or caregiver so everyone sees the same action cards and logs. Consistent care across caregivers makes the biggest difference — even when everyone speaks a different language.

🔒

Your notes are private. Always.

Everything you share — including texts from teachers or emails from therapists you paste in — is only visible to you. We will never share, sell, or use your data for anything other than helping you.

How it works
1

Tell the app what's on your mind

Share any challenge you've been noticing with your child — no need to frame it as a "goal." Just talk or type freely. "He won't share toys," "She doesn't respond when I call her name."

💡 You can speak directly using the 🎤 button — no typing needed.
2

Get goals & action cards, instantly

The app turns your input into therapy-style goals and generates practical action cards — specific steps you can try at home today, built around what your child needs.

💡 Review, edit, or remove any suggested goal before getting started.
3

Try it & share your feedback

Tap an action card to see the full steps. After trying something, tap "Tap for more & updates" and share how it went — what you tried, what worked, what didn't. You can also include feedback from your therapist or teacher.

💡 Speak freely — the app will organize your input automatically.
4

Get updated action cards

Based on your feedback, the app suggests improved action steps — building on what worked and moving away from what didn't. Your action cards evolve with your child.

5

Invite family members

Go to Settings → Caregivers to invite a co-parent, grandparent, or other caregiver. They'll see the same goals and logs, so everyone is aligned — even if they speak a different language.

💡 Each caregiver can set their own language preference independently.
6

Generate reports for your providers

Before an IEP meeting, therapist session, or teacher conference — go to Insights → Generate a report to share. Choose the purpose, select what to include, and send it directly by email.

💡 Reports are customizable. You control what gets shared with who.
Tab guide
Home

Your action cards. Tap any card to view full steps, add a note, and update your plan.

Journal

Anything on your mind — vent, share a therapist note, or log a general observation. AI will organize it for you.

Insights

See your recent activity, goal progress, and generate reports to share with providers.

Settings

Manage your goals, invite caregivers, change language, and access your account.

🔒

Your data is private. Everything you share stays within your account. Therapy Buddy never sells your data or shares it with third parties. Each caregiver can only see data for the child they're connected to.
