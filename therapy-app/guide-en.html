Parenting a child with special needs is one of the hardest things there is — the appointments, IEP meetings, therapist homework. Therapy Buddy was built for the six days between sessions, when it's just you and your child.

"Over 5 years of therapy, my older son progressed quickly because I stayed engaged. With my younger son, I got busy — lost track of goals, didn't know what to do. Progress stalled. I built this app so no parent has to go through that. Since using it, my son has shown meaningful progress."

— Sera Koo, mom of two & Therapy Buddy builder

What it does for you
💭

Vent it out — we turn it into a plan

Talk or type freely about any challenge. We turn your concern into specific, home-based action steps your child's therapist would approve of.

✨

Know what's working — and what to try next

Tap a card after trying something and share how it went. Your action cards update automatically based on what works for your child.

👨‍👩‍👧

Keep the whole family aligned

Invite a co-parent, grandparent, or caregiver. Everyone sees the same goals and logs — even if they speak a different language.

📋

Walk into every meeting prepared

Generate a progress report before any IEP, therapy check-in, or teacher conference — and share it directly by email.
